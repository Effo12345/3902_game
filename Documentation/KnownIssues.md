# Known issues
- Moving into a wall at high speeds will occasionally cause Mario to float on the corner of a block.
- Colliding with the floor will occasionally cancel all of Mario's horizontal momentum.
- Interacting with the top of enemies at a low angle sometimes forces them through the floor.
- Mario can force enemies into solid surfaces by running into them.
- Mario can phase through moving enemies.
- Larger mario sprites clip through the ground.
- Spamming jump against a wall sometimes allows Mario to scale it.
