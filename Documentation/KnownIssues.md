# Known issues
- Colliding with the floor at high vertical speeds will occasionally cancel all of Mario's horizontal momentum (but less often now!).
- Spamming jump against a wall sometimes allows Mario to scale it.
- All entities are ticked globally, meaning entities will often jump into a pit before you can reach them.
- Collecting powerups causes strange offsets in Mario's position.
- All states use the big Mario sprite for the peace sign. It's shockingly hard to find the small mario sprite for that.
- The level music doesn't loop seamlessly.
- Mario doesn't display the starman color switching correctly.
- The timer expiring doesn't kill the player.
- Blocks don't produce debris on player contact.
- Minecraft mushrooms will pass directly through question blocks.
